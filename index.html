About Us

Empowering members through secure saving and lending since 2024.

Our History

Liquidity SACCO Society Limited was established in September 2024 by three visionary members who shared a common desire to build a financially empowered community through collective saving, responsible lending, and mutual welfare. Guided by the slogan "Together Making Lives Better", the founders envisioned a member-centered SACCO providing accessible financial solutions while fostering integrity, transparency, accountability, and professionalism.

From humble beginnings with only three members, Liquidity SACCO has grown to twelve active members, reflecting confidence in its vision.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries.

1. How do I join the SACCO?
You can join using the online registration portal. Provide your ID, passport photo, and a minimum initial deposit of KSh 300. Or contact our Treasurer via 0740556252.
2. What is the interest rate on loans?
Interest rates vary: 10% for a soft loan (10 days) and 20% for a usual loan (1 month repayment).
3. How do I check the loan I qualify for?
Simply key in your total savings in our calculator above — you can get up to 75% of that amount as your loan eligibility.
